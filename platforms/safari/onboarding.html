Explore Finds

Finds the songs in the posts you scroll past on Instagram Explore, and turns them into a Spotify playlist you can play right here.

  • Use instagram.com in Safari, not the Instagram app.
  • Songs are picked up while you scroll. Nothing is posted or changed on Instagram.
  • You need Spotify Premium.

Your Spotify app

Explore Finds talks to Spotify through a free developer app that you own. If you set up Explore Finds on your computer, use the same app.

Don't have a Spotify app yet?
  1. On a computer (easier) or here, open Create app and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. Any name and description. Add the Redirect URI above, tick Web API, agree to the terms, and Save.
  3. Copy the Client ID from the app's page and paste it above.

Connect Spotify

Spotify's login opens in a new tab. When you've agreed, it closes by itself; come back to this tab.

Allow Instagram

Safari asks before an extension can read a website. Explore Finds needs instagram.com.

  1. Open instagram.com in Safari and log in.
  2. Tap the extensions button in the address bar, then Explore Finds.
  3. Choose Always Allow on This Website.

You're set

Open Explore and scroll. Songs collect by themselves; the number on the Explore Finds button shows how many so far.

Tap that button any time to see the list, play songs, and build your playlist.
