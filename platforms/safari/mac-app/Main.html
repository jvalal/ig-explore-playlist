Explore Playlist Gen

The songs from your Instagram Explore feed, in a Spotify playlist.

Turn on the extension in Safari to start.

  1. Click Open Safari Settings below.
  2. In Extensions, tick Explore Playlist Gen.
  3. Come back to this window.

The extension is on in Safari.

  1. Open Instagram Explore in Safari and scroll as usual.
  2. Click the button in Safari's toolbar to see the songs.
  3. The first time, it walks you through connecting Spotify.

No data collection. No auto-scrolling. Not affiliated with Instagram, Meta or Spotify.
